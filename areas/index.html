Historic harbour capital

Willemstad

Willemstad combines the waterfront districts of Punda and Otrobanda with city hotels, private apartments and a working port. Book using the actual property, not only the city name.

Jan Thiel

Jan Thiel is a favourite base for holiday apartments, private villas and beach days. A massage at your accommodation can make the afternoon quieter without another cross-island trip.

The Mambo Beach area is associated with a lively seaside setting, resorts and restaurants. Private massage requests work best back at a suitable room, not automatically on the sand.
