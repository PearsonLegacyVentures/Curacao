What to expect

Not every Curaçao beach is suitable for a massage table. Sand, winds, privacy, property rules and a covered backup must be checked before booking. A professional massage requires a suitable private room, enough space for a portable table and the therapist's access to the property or vessel.

Where can this be arranged?

Requests for Jan Thiel, Willemstad, Blue Bay and other parts of Curaçao depend on local provider coverage and the actual accommodation. Share the property or vessel, exact area and preferred time.

Booking requirements

  • Provide the hotel, villa, apartment or yacht name.
  • Confirm the host or resort permits outside wellness providers.
  • Choose a 60- or 90-minute preference and a realistic time window.
  • Allow the team to review therapist eligibility and travel before confirmation.
