CURAÇAO · PRIVACY

How we use your enquiry information.

Your booking details are used to respond to the specific service you request.

Information collected

We collect the name, email, phone, property or vessel name, destination, requested treatment, preferred date and time, and voluntary booking notes that you submit.

Purpose

This information is used to respond, evaluate local provider availability and access, give a final quote and coordinate the appointment if you accept.

Where it goes

Secure forms send enquiries to the Tranquilitas business inbox using an email service when configured. If delivery fails, you may send the request manually through WhatsApp or email instead.

Sharing and retention

Relevant details may be shared with appropriate local staff or potential providers solely to evaluate or deliver a request. We do not sell booking details. Correspondence is retained for operational and legitimate business purposes, subject to applicable law.

Contact

For a privacy question, email info@massagebahamas.com. This site does not take online payment or embed advertising tracking scripts.
